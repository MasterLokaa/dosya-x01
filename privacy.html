Privacy Policy

Dosya X01 Yükleyici. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Dosya X01 Yükleyici is a desktop application used by the owner of the Dosya X01 channel to upload their own videos to their own accounts. It runs on the owner's computer. It has no server and no user database.

Information the app uses

  • Basic TikTok account information, such as the open ID and the privacy options of the connected account, provided through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Google after the account owner gives permission.
  • Video files, titles and descriptions chosen by the account owner.

How it is used

This information is used only to connect the account and to upload the selected video when the account owner starts an upload.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party. Videos and their text are sent only to the platform selected for upload. No data is sold or used for advertising.

Removing access

The connection can be removed at any time inside the app or from the TikTok account settings. Removing it deletes the stored token from the computer.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Dosya X01 YouTube channel.
